## About Redmine Kanban

Redmine Kanban is a board plugin for managing Redmine issues as cards. It uses HTML5 Canvas to render the board and helps you track progress and spot stalled work.

## Supported Environments

Redmine Kanban supports Redmine 6.0, 6.1, and 7.0 series along with all modern desktop browsers. See the [Introduction](/en/guide/introduction) for requirements, or head directly to [Installation and setup](/en/guide/installation).
